# Supplier Directory & Bridge Plan — Goods Tenders

**Date:** June 10, 2026
**Companion file:** `goods-tender-catalog.psv` — 194 open-bidding goods tenders, pipe-delimited,
grouped into 11 categories. Notice URL for any row =
`https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/tender-notice/<reference in lowercase>`.

**The arbitrage model:** Government buys at bid price; we source at dealer/distributor cost.
The spread (typically 8–15% on commodities, 20–30% on niche items) is our margin. Award
results are public (winner + price), so every loss teaches the exact price-to-beat next time.

> Supplier names below are a starting directory of known Canadian/North-American
> distributors per category — verify current dealer programs, territory coverage and resale
> terms before outreach. Names marked ✓ were verified via current web sources.

## Bridge plan — how we connect the two sides and take margin

### Week 1–2: Foundations
1. PBN + CanadaBuys/SAP Ariba registration (also unlocks bid submission).
2. Open distributor/reseller accounts in the 2–3 lanes chosen with the dealer partner
   (Grainger/Fastenal/NAPA accounts are immediate; IT distribution accounts need a business
   number and take ~1–2 weeks; OEM dealer agreements take longer — start now).
3. Set CanadaBuys alerts per category keywords; AI re-runs the full catalog scan weekly.

### Week 2–4: First bids (low-risk commodity lane)
4. Bid 5–10 DND commodity RFPs (VEHICLE_PARTS + material handling): pull the RFP's item
   list/part numbers → dealer quotes cost + freight to depot (Edmonton/Montréal/Halifax/
   Esquimalt) → add 10–15% → compliance check → submit. Expect early losses; harvest the
   published award prices to calibrate.
5. Simultaneously bid the open **standing offers** with longer runway: Aluminium Extrusions
   (Jun 23), Flags (Jun 25), Vests (Jul 9), Parkas/Trousers (Jul 20), Environmental Control
   Units (Jul 8), Small Arms Cleaning Kits (Jul 17 — verify no CGP requirement).

### Month 2–6: Build the durable position
6. Qualify into goods RFSAs matching the dealer's lines: Forklifts (Dec 2026), Riding Mowers
   (Nov 2026), HDMS shelving (Dec 2026), Office Seating (Apr 2027), trailers (2028+),
   Electrical products (Nov 2028), Lab equipment (2035).
7. Sign OEM authorization letters where needed (headsets, AV, ATV/UTV, outboards).
8. Cash-flow plan: Canada pays net-30 after delivery/invoice; secure supplier terms (net-45/60)
   or a working-capital line so we never fund inventory long.

### Gates checked on every bid (hard rules)
- Controlled Goods? → skip unless registered.
- Canadian content / Reciprocal Procurement clause? → confirm supply chain qualifies.
- OEM authorization letter required? → attach or skip.
- PSIB conditional set-aside? → note the risk of competition being limited to Indigenous firms.
- Delivery deadline & depot achievable? → dealer confirms before we price.
